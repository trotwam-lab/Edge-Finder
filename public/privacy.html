Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

What we collect

EdgeFinder collects account information you provide, subscription status, saved preferences, and betting records you choose to track. The service also receives standard technical data such as device, browser, IP address, and request logs needed for security and reliability.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate the odds and analytics tools, process subscriptions, preserve user settings, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and improve the service.

Service providers

EdgeFinder relies on vendors including Firebase for authentication and data storage, Stripe for billing, Vercel for hosting, and sports-data providers for market information. Those providers process data under their own privacy terms.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to run EdgeFinder, when required by law, or when you direct us to share it. Community sharing is optional and off by default.

Retention and control

We retain information while your account is active and as needed for security, billing, legal, and operational purposes. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

Security and age

We use reasonable safeguards but no online service can guarantee absolute security. EdgeFinder is intended only for adults age 21 or older in jurisdictions where sports betting is legal.

Contact

Email admin@edgefinderdaily.com with privacy questions or requests.
